CSR GROUP OF COMPANIES - IMAGE UPLOAD DIRECTORY LIST
========================================================================

Drop your image files into these folders to have them render directly in
the frontend.

HOW IT WORKS:
1. When you place a file (e.g. `milk.jpg`) in the matching folder, the
   website immediately displays your uploaded image!
2. If any image has not yet been uploaded, the frontend automatically 
   uses the high-resolution online fallback photo so your website never
   shows a broken image.
